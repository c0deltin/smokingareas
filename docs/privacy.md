# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 12 November 2026*

This policy explains which personal data the Ippuku app ("the app")
processes, why, and what rights you have. The app is designed to work with
as little personal data as possible: you can use it without an account, and
your location never leaves your device.

## 2. Using the app without an account

**Location.** The app uses your device's location to show your position on
the map and – if you switch alerts on – to notify you when you come close to
a smoking area. The location is processed **only on your device**. It is not
sent to us or stored on a server. You can revoke the location permission in
your device settings at any time.

**Settings and cached data** (alert radius, downloaded map data, the city
you looked at) are stored only on your device.

**Notifications** are created locally on your device; no push service is
used.

**Map display (Google Maps).** The base map is provided by Google Maps
(Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland).
To load the map, your device connects to Google's servers, which receive
your IP address and the map area being displayed. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f)
GDPR (legitimate interest in showing a map). Google may process data in the
USA. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
and the [Google Maps terms](https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/).

**Data updates.** The app downloads updates of the official and
OpenStreetMap data from GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA), which receives your IP
address. Only if no data is available on your device does the app query the
public OpenStreetMap Overpass servers directly. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f)
GDPR.

**Community content.** Places, ratings and photos contributed by other users
are loaded from our server (see section 4); this request includes your IP
address.

## 3. Account (optional)

To report places, rate, add photos, vote or report closures you need an
account. You can sign in with **Apple** (iPhone) or **Google**.

When you sign in, we receive from Apple or Google an identifier for your
account, your e-mail address (with Apple possibly a relay address) and your
name, if you share it. We store:

- your account identifier and e-mail address (only used to manage your
  account; never shown to other users),
- your display name – by default only your **first name** – which is shown
  next to your contributions,
- your contributions: reported places (position, name, description and
  attributes), ratings and comments, photos, "still there / gone" votes and
  closure reports, each with the time it was made,
- users you blocked and content you reported (reports are only visible to
  us),
- technical data for operating the service, such as sign-in timestamps and
  security logs including IP addresses.

**Photos** are re-encoded on your device before upload; metadata such as the
GPS position, camera model or capture time is removed.

**Visibility.** Contributions are public: anyone using the app can see them
together with your display name.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the community features you
request) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (security, preventing abuse, moderation).

**Retention and deletion.** Your data is stored as long as your account
exists. You can delete your account in the app at any time (Settings →
Account → Delete account). This deletes your account, ratings, photos,
votes, closure reports, blocks and reports. Places you reported stay on the
map **without** your name, as they are useful to others. Security logs are
kept only as long as the hosting provider's retention periods require.

For sign-in, the privacy policies of
[Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and
[Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) also apply.

## 4. Hosting and processors

The community data is stored with Supabase, Inc. (USA) on servers in Tokyo,
Japan (Amazon Web Services). Supabase processes the data on our behalf under
a data processing agreement. Transfers to countries outside the EU/EEA are
based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the
EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework; Japan is recognised by the EU as providing
an adequate level of data protection.

## 5. No tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools. We do
not sell or share personal data for advertising.

## 6. Moderation

To keep the community content useful and lawful, we review reported content
and may remove content or block accounts that violate the
[terms of use](../terms/).

## 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification
(Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data
portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate
interests (Art. 21). To exercise these rights, contact us at the address in
section 1. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data
protection supervisory authority. Users in Japan have corresponding rights
under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI).

## 8. Children

The app is not directed at children. Contributing requires an Apple or
Google account and is intended for adults.

## 9. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The
current version is always available on this page.
