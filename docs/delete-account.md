# Delete your smokingareas account

You can delete your account and your data at any time.

## In the app

1. Open the menu (⋮) → **Settings** → **Account**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

## Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, send an e-mail to
[CONTACT E-MAIL] with the subject "Delete my account" and the name shown
with your contributions. We delete the account within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- your account (sign-in with Apple or Google) and display name,
- your ratings and comments, photos, "still there / gone" votes, closure
  reports, blocks and content reports.

Places you reported stay on the map **without** your name, because other
users rely on them. Security logs of the hosting provider are kept only as
long as its retention periods require.

See also the [privacy policy](../privacy/).
