## English (primary)

**Subtitle [30]:** Designated areas & toilets

**Promotional text [170]:**
Smoking on the street is banned in many Japanese cities. Find the nearest
designated smoking area or public toilet – official data, OSM and community.

**Keywords [100]:**
smoking area,designated,japan,tokyo,osaka,kyoto,toilet,restroom,wc,map,travel,ashtray,rules,fine

**Description [4000]:**

In many Japanese cities smoking on the street is banned and can be fined –
smoking is only allowed in designated smoking areas. smokingareas shows you
where they are, together with public toilets, in 54 major cities from
Sapporo to Naha.

ONE MAP, ALL SOURCES
• Official lists of the cities and wards (Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, Okayama …)
• OpenStreetMap data for every covered city
• Places reported by the community
Duplicates are merged into one marker. Official entries are marked with a
gold ring.

USEFUL DETAILS
• Indoor smoking room or outdoor area, covered, opening hours, fees
• Access notes, e.g. "station platform – ticket only"
• Public toilets with wheelchair access and baby changing tables
• Walking directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps

OPTIONAL ALERT
Turn on alerts and get a notification when you come close to a designated
smoking area – the radius is up to you (10–150 m). Your location is
processed only on your device and never sent anywhere.

COMMUNITY
Sign in with Apple or Google to report missing places, rate them, add photos
and mark places as gone or temporarily closed. If enough people report a
place as gone, it disappears from the map. Browsing works without an
account.

PRIVACY FIRST
No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Only your first name is shown with your
contributions, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.

Please always follow local rules and signs on site.

Data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); official open data of Japanese
cities and wards (CC BY 4.0).

**What's new (first release):**

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Data used to track you: **No.** Tracking: **No.**

Data linked to the user (all for **App Functionality** only):

| Category | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Info | Name | first name shown with contributions (from Apple/Google sign-in) |
| Contact Info | Email Address | account management (from Apple/Google sign-in; may be a relay address) |
| Identifiers | User ID | account identifier |
| User Content | Photos or Videos | photos users add to places |
| User Content | Other User Content | reported places, ratings and comments, votes, closure and content reports |

**Not collected:** Location (processed only on the device; a reported
place's position is user content, not the user's location), Health, Financial
Info, Contacts, Browsing/Search History, Purchases, Usage Data, Diagnostics
(the app has no analytics or crash reporting of its own).

Third-party SDKs: check the current data disclosures of the **Google Maps
SDK for iOS** and **Google Sign-In** (Google's developer documentation lists
what they collect, typically diagnostics/performance data that is not linked
to the user) and add those entries as Google states them.

## App Review information

Sign-in required: **No** (browsing works without an account). Contact:
your name, phone and e-mail.

**Notes for the reviewer** (paste into "Notes"):

> smokingareas helps visitors and residents follow Japan's smoking rules:
> many cities ban smoking on the street and fine violations, so smoking is
> only allowed in designated smoking areas. The app shows these areas and
> public toilets from official city open data and OpenStreetMap. It does not
> sell, advertise or promote tobacco products.
>
> No account is needed to use the map. Community features (report a place,
> rate, add photos, vote) use Sign in with Apple (or Google); any Apple ID
> works, so no demo account is needed.
>
> Background location ("Always") is only used for the optional proximity
> alert: when the user turns on alerts, the app notifies them when they come
> close to a designated smoking area (radius set in Settings). The location
> is processed on the device only and never sent to a server. Without
> "Always" the app works normally; alerts then only work while it is open.
>
> User-generated content: every place, rating and photo has a menu to
> report it or block its author; reports are reviewed by us within 24 hours
> and objectionable content is removed. Users accept the terms of use when
> signing in. Accounts can be deleted in the app (Settings → Account →
> Delete account).
>
> To test: allow location (or pick a city via the title, e.g. "Tokyo"),
> tap a green marker to see a smoking area, a blue one for a toilet. Tap
> "Report a spot" (bottom right) to try the community features.

Note: Apple expects reported content to be handled **within 24 hours**
(Guideline 1.2) – check `content_reports` in the backend dashboard daily.
